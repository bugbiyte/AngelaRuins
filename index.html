About Me

Annngie is a Bay Area transplant from L.A. who has spent the better part of two decades championing music and the people who make it — contributing editorial content to MP3.com, CBS Radio's Live 105 & KROQ, and Last.fm.

These days she’s a QA lead for a software team, front-end tinkerer, and creative problem-solver. She also hosts a monthly vinyl DJ show Crossfade on HydeFM.
