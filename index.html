About Me

Anngie is a Bay Area transplant from L.A. who has spent the better part of two decades championing music and the people who make it — contributing editorial content in the early aughts to the legendary MP3.com, CBS Radio's Live 105 & KROQ, and Last.fm.

These days, she works in tech as a QA bug wrangler with a focus in Software Web Development. When not chasing bugs, she's prepping vinyl sets for her montly Hydefm Radio show called Crossfade, a show where she and guest DJs explore the darker corners of the electronic spectrum.
